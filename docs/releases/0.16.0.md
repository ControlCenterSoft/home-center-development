# Home Center 0.16.0

Cumulative portable compute release: the 0.15 inventory/discovery/profile foundation plus a runtime-discovered Proxmox provider, VM/LXC plan-only lifecycle and provider-neutral resource placement.

The compute boundary is fail-closed: malformed or inconsistent observations are rejected, a guest must fit a single eligible node, and an HA guest requires two eligible nodes with failover capacity. Lifecycle and placement results never grant production mutation authority.

Home Center 0.16.0 is cumulative over the 0.15.0 inventory, discovery and deployment-profile foundation and preserves its infrastructure-neutral safety boundaries.

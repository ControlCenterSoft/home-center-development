# Home Center 0.61.0

Status: source development; not Release Candidate and not Public Stable.

## Назначение

0.61.0 развивает VPN egress provider/policy поверх родительского Internet/DNS/Proxy policy layer. VPN не является обходным каналом: более строгий parental `DENY` всегда имеет приоритет над VPN и direct egress.

## Подготовленная source-граница

- provider-neutral VPN candidate evidence: provider/version/location, reachability, latency, stability and supported capabilities;
- explicit DNS strategy: `provider`, `parental` or `system`;
- bounded split-routing domain set;
- kill-switch and fallback semantics with fail-closed contradiction checks;
- exact binding to composed Household policy and optional parental Internet policy;
- deterministic provider selection and route decision;
- strict storage/transport reconstruction of provider, policy, observation and route-decision evidence;
- Cozy and Full UI projections without execution authority.

## Safety boundary

The current source scope does not configure a VPN client, routing table, DNS service, firewall or external provider. It does not grant infrastructure mutation or external publication authority.

Provider selection is not VPN activation. Provider observation is read-only evidence. A future mutation path must use a typed provider adapter, durable Job/Audit, exact state revalidation, a pre-mutation recovery boundary and mandatory post-condition read-back. Ambiguous provider outcomes must not be retried automatically.

Parental filtering remains authoritative. A VPN policy cannot turn `vpn_allowed=false` into true. When parental policy is bound, system DNS cannot be selected and the policy requires kill switch plus deny fallback.

## Release boundary

0.61.0 is not assigned a release identity by this source preparation. `VERSION`, package/runtime version and Public Stable identity remain unchanged until a separate exact candidate step.

Before Release Candidate/Public Stable, the exact candidate must qualify the current source on its final immutable identity, including security/privacy/infrastructure-neutrality, reproducible packaging, supported install/upgrade/rollback and complete release artifacts. Concrete VPN provider execution or HA support must be separately qualified before being claimed; otherwise those capabilities remain disabled/unclaimed.

## Commercial / legal boundary

The provider-neutral policy model introduces no concrete bundled VPN provider and makes no commercial-launch claim. Any future concrete VPN provider requires separate license/source/distribution/notice/support evidence before commercial claims are made.
